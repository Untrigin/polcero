We have signed our first orders for robots built around a specific process at an industrial client. The client buys a finished, commissioned station together with documentation and service - not a catalogue machine that has to fit their process.

The starting point is our own solutions and prototypes, adapted to a specific purpose. This is the essence of the modular approach: we do not design each order from scratch, but we also do not sell a machine that must fit the client's process. We fit the technology to the process, not the other way around.

Deployments are planned for early 2027 and are financed from our own funds and from client orders. This stage does not require external capital, because we build robots to specific orders, not for stock.
